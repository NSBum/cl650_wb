About

Challenger 650 Weight and Balance Calculator

This web application provides weight and balance calculations for the Bombardier Challenger 650 aircraft. It allows pilots and flight planners to input crew weights, passenger counts, baggage, and fuel loads to determine the aircraft's takeoff weight (TOW) and center of gravity position as a percentage of Mean Aerodynamic Chord (%MAC).

Features

  • Calculate Operating Empty Weight (OEW), Zero Fuel Weight (ZFW), and Takeoff Weight (TOW)
  • Determine Center of Gravity (CG) position and %MAC
  • Visual weight and balance chart with operational envelope
  • Support for both Imperial (lbs) and Metric (kg) units
  • Automatic collision detection for chart labels

Technical Details

The application uses standard weight and balance calculations based on aircraft-specific stations and reference data. All calculations are performed server-side to ensure accuracy and consistency.

For detailed information, refer to the Challenger 650 Weight and Balance Manual (PDF).
